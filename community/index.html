Chapter three

The trip lasts three days.
The community lasts longer than that.

The chat usually starts on the bus. By the ride home, people are swapping pictures, teasing the ones who struggled, and asking when Zico is dropping the next date.

The trips so far

Every one of these happened.
You should have been there.

Hike Adakluto 202516 people

The first climb everyone remembered.

Early call time. Long ride. Most people had barely met. Halfway up the hill the jokes started, and by the time they came down nobody felt like a stranger anymore.

That climb turned a bus full of strangers into a real group.

Asenema Waterfalls 202514 people

The one with no flyer.

No flyer. No Instagram post. Zico just texted his people. The spots filled quickly, and it became obvious that the crowd was already there, ready to show up.

By then, the crowd was already there. It only needed a date.

Ada / Nkyinkyim MuseumMuseum day

The day that moved at a gentler pace.

Ada slowed the pace without lowering the energy. Museum grounds first, then water, then portraits, then the conversations that only happen when nobody is checking the time.

A reminder that the quieter days can stay with people just as hard.

Akwamu Gorge 202520 people

The hike nobody expected.

The gorge hike was hard and everybody said so. They still kept climbing, got the view at the top, and came home with the pictures that made everyone else ask what they missed.

After Akwamu, a lot more people started paying attention.

Keta 3-Day Trip / December 202533 people

The biggest one.

Christmas season. Keta. Beach camp, fort stop, house-party energy, and 33 people who started as strangers and left with something much closer than that.

Fastest sell-out. Biggest energy. Still one of the loudest stories in the community.

Togo 2026Newest trip

The first border chapter brought back its own mood.

Three days over the line and the energy shifted immediately. Lome gave the trip its own pace: landmark stops, portraits, road moments, and the feeling that the community had properly stretched beyond Ghana.

That was the moment the border chapter stopped being an idea.
