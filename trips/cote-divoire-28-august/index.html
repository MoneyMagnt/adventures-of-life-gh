Abidjan and beyond

City mornings, coastlines and culture.
Four places, one crew.

Abidjan is the base, with time planned across Grand-Bassam, Assinie and Yamoussoukro. Expect a mix of city energy, historic stops, coastal views and the everyday culture that makes crossing the border worth it.

The journey begins overnight, so most of the long drive passes while the group sleeps. By morning, the border formalities are the last step before the experience properly begins.
