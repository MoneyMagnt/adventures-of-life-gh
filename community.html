Hike Adakluto 202516 people

The first climb everyone remembered.

Early call time. Long ride. Most people had barely met. Then the climb started and the group had to earn the top together. By the time they came down, the crew already felt different.

The trip that proved the route mattered as much as the people.

Asenema Waterfalls 202514 people

The one with no flyer.

No flyer. No Instagram post. Zico just texted his people. The spots filled quickly, and the trip made it obvious that the community was already there waiting for a real route to say yes to.

The trip that showed the audience was real before the brand even caught up.

Ada / Nkyinkyim MuseumMuseum day

The day that moved at a gentler pace.

Ada gave the group a different kind of memory. Museum grounds first, then the move toward the water, then portraits and quieter conversation before the ride back. No rush. Just a day with room in it.

The trip that proved not every unforgettable day has to be a hard climb.

Akwamu Gorge 202520 people

The hike nobody expected.

The gorge hike was hard and everybody said so. But they kept going, got the view at the top, and came back with the kind of pictures that pull new people toward the next trip.

The trip that made Adventures of Life visible to a wider crowd.

Keta 3-Day Trip / December 202533 people

The biggest one.

Christmas season. Keta. Beach camp, fort stop, house-party energy, and 33 people who started as strangers and left with something much closer than that.

The fastest sell-out. The most energy. Still one of the loudest stories in the community.

Togo 2026Newest trip

Three days over the line and the energy shifted immediately. Lome gave the trip its own pace: landmark stops, portraits, road moments, and the feeling that the community had properly stretched beyond Ghana.

The trip that made the border chapter feel lived, not imagined.
